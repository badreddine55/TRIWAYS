## Cluster handling

- Keep synonyms and close variations together on the most suitable page. “Fret maritime” and “transport maritime” need not become duplicate pages.
- Separate an educational explanation from a service page only where it answers a genuinely different need.
- Route and local terms are conditional on real coverage. Do not turn every city or country variation into an almost identical page.
- Test brand + Maroc queries separately from generic freight queries to study entity confusion.
- Exclude employment, passenger transport, tracking for unrelated carriers, personal relocation and consumer shopping from the initial B2B shortlist unless those services are genuinely offered. This is an organic prioritization decision, not a paid-ad negative-keyword setup.
