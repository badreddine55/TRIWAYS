## Exports to collect

1. Select the property that covers `triwaysslogistics.com` and its live canonical host. Confirm ownership in your own account.
2. Open Search results / Performance. Select Web search and enable clicks, impressions, CTR and average position.
3. Export the latest three complete months and the preceding equal-length period. Use the longest available period separately to detect seasonality; do not mix it into the short-period comparison.
4. Export Queries and Pages with country = Morocco. Repeat without a country filter to see international demand. Keep these datasets separate.
5. For important queries, filter by the query and inspect Pages. For important landing pages, filter by the page and inspect Queries. Independent Queries and Pages exports do not provide a reliable query-to-page join.
6. Export country and device views for context. Record the exact filters and export date. Include the page-indexing report and URL Inspection screenshots for key service URLs if technical diagnosis is also wanted.
7. If your account exposes a Generative AI performance report, export it separately with its available dimensions. Do not label ordinary Web search clicks as ChatGPT or Perplexity traffic.

## Decision rules — proposals, not findings

- Start with non-brand queries that already show impressions and match a genuine service. Review the landing page and live results before choosing an action.
- Average positions around 4–20 can be a useful review queue, but they are aggregated observations, not stable rank promises.
- Review high-impression, low-CTR queries relative to comparable queries, device and position. Do not apply one universal CTR target.
- Several URLs receiving impressions for a query do not automatically prove cannibalization. Check intent and whether the pages complement each other.
- A missing query can reflect limited visibility or reporting limits. Do not interpret it as zero market demand.
- CTR = clicks / impressions; calculate only when impressions > 0. Compare absolute click differences and percentage change only when the baseline permits it.
- GSC impressions concern your site, not total keyword search volume. A `site:` search is not a complete indexing report.
