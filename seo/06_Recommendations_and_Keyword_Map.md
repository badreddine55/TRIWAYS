This is an access limitation, not proof of deindexing or of a React rendering failure. ## Competitive research takeaways — analyst interpretation

The five reference sites in file 02 separate services or modes and provide quotation routes. Use that as a benchmark for clarity, not a reason to copy their wording or capabilities. A useful Triways page should explain what is handled, where it is handled, what the buyer must provide, what is excluded and how to request a quote. Add genuine evidence such as approved case examples and verifiable credentials when available.

## SEO and GEO actions

- Confirm the exact company name, canonical domain and contact details. Use the same verified entity information across the website and owned profiles. Address same-name confusion through truthful Morocco context, not repeated keyword stuffing.
- Inspect important pages in Search Console and compare what a crawler retrieves with the rendered page. Check metadata, indexability, internal links and sitemap accuracy. Do not start a framework migration based solely on this extraction result.
- Use a distinct purpose and useful content for each service page. Answer real buyer questions in readable text, with relevant sources and review dates.
- Include valid structured data only when it matches visible, verified facts. It is not an AI inclusion guarantee.
- Seek genuine relevant business listings and partner references. Do not buy fabricated reviews or manufacture Reddit endorsements.
- Improve quote forms around the information needed to assess an actual shipment. Track qualified submissions, not only raw traffic.

## Suggested AI-discovery test prompts

These are test inputs, not evidence that any engine currently recommends Triways:

- « Quelles entreprises proposent du transport national et international de marchandises au Maroc ? »
- « Je cherche un prestataire de groupage maritime au Maroc. Comment comparer les offres ? »
- « Quelle entreprise contacter pour transporter des palettes au Maroc ? »
- « Que fait Triways au Maroc et quel est son site officiel ? »
- “Which Morocco freight forwarders handle small business shipments?”

For each run, record platform, model if shown, search mode, language, date, full answer, cited URLs and whether the correct domain appears. Repeat a fixed set at a consistent interval; do not generalize from one response. Branded prompts test identity recognition; unbranded prompts test discovery. Neither guarantees future inclusion.
